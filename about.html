፠ Institutional Journey ፠

Eight+ Years Shaping Ethiopian Screen Talent

Ecosystem & Diplomatic Collaborations

Connected to Ethiopia’s Leading Cultural & Film Institutions

Guma Award (ጉማ አዋርድ)

Official 3-film annual direct competition quota backed by free college equipment and faculty mentorship.

Alliance Éthio-Française & Goethe-Institut

Co-hosted Obscura Cinema student screenings (with Nada Hubs & White Canvas) and Goethe documentary/grant forums.

Addis Ababa Culture & Cinema Bureaus

Partnered on Cinema Empire heritage screenings of "Hirut Abatwa Man New?" and formalist critiques of 35mm classic "Aster".

Endrias Film Company (Sister Studio)

Commercial production partner producing documentaries, TVCs, music videos, corporate films, and 3D animation.

Ethiopian Academy of Sciences & INSA

Active student participation in national TV-drama dialogues and INSA Cyber Security Awareness short-film contests.

Babul Kheyr Humanitarian Outreach

Graduating classes organize community service and fundraising initiatives alongside zero-fee job placement.
